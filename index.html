📦 Agricultural Inputs

Supply of subsidized chemical fertilizers (Urea, DAP, Potash), quality seeds, bio-fertilizers, and farm machinery rentals.

Governance Structure & Officers

General Body

Supreme authority consisting of all eligible members of the cooperative society.

Board of Directors

Elected democratically by members for a tenure of 5 years to manage society affairs.

President / Secretary

Chief elected executive and paid chief executive officer responsible for operations.

Statutory Auditor

Conducts annual statutory audit under Section 80 of the TN Cooperative Societies Act.
